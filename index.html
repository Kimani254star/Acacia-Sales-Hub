Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

Can I turn a quote into an order?

Yes. Quotes move on to sales orders, and sales orders to invoices.

Can I record payments?

Yes. Payments are recorded against invoices so you can see what is still owed.

Can I handle returns?

Yes. There is a Sales Returns area for goods that come back.

Is my data kept per company?

Yes. Each company has its own separate data, and everyone in that company sees the same records.
